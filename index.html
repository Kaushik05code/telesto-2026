The Noctis Singularity

Telesto is the moment the noise resolves — where markets, chaos and competition collapse into a single point of clarity.

Since 2017, Telesto has been a proving ground for the sharpest business minds in the country and beyond. CUSBMA's flagship has grown into an international-level business fest where intellect meets resilience and ideas are tested under the pressure of simulated corporate realities.

Hosted at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bannerghatta Road Campus, Telesto 2026 draws teams from campuses nationwide and universities abroad — all competing under a single law: in all disorder, a secret order.
