The Noctis Singularity

Telesto is the moment the noise resolves — where markets, chaos and competition collapse into a single point of clarity.

The Noctis Singularity is CUSBMA's flagship management fest: two days where business acumen meets cosmic order. Case cracks, quizzes and live gauntlets pull the sharpest student minds toward one event horizon — the champion's title.

Hosted at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bannerghatta Road Campus, Telesto 2026 gathers teams from across the country to compete under a single law: in all disorder, a secret order.
