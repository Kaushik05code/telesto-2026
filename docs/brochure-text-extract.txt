ABOUT CHRIST
Founded on the educational vision of 19th-century
educationalist and social reformer St. Kuriakose Elias
Chavara, CHRIST (Deemed to be University) originated as
Christ College in 1969. Administered by the Carmelites of
Mary Immaculate (CMI), the university has been a pioneer
in Indian higher education, introducing modern curricula,
enforcing academic discipline and promoting holistic
education through innovative practices and dedicated
staff.
The University Grants Commission (UGC) of India granted
autonomy to Christ College in 2004 and recognized it as
an Institution with Potential for Excellence in 2006. In 2008,
the Ministry of Human Resource Development of the
Government of India officially declared the institution a
"Deemed to be University”, renaming it as CHRIST
(Deemed to be University

TELESTO 2026
Telesto is an annual International-level business fest
hosted by Christ University School of Business and
Management Association, Bannerghatta Road Campus.
Designed for management students, the event mimics
real-world corporate challenges to test participants skills
running from September 9th and 10th.

Since its inception in 2017, Telesto has been more than a
fest, it has been a proving ground for some of the sharpest
business minds in the country and beyond. Over the past
decade, it has evolved into a flagship international-level
business competition of Christ University's School of
Business and Management (Bannerghatta Road Campus),
where intellect meets resilience, and ideas are tested
under the pressure of simulated corporate realities.

ABOUT THE THEME
The Noctis Singularity "In all chaos there is a cosmos, in all
disorder a secret order."
The Noctis Singularity draws inspiration from two
extraordinary celestial phenomena the eclipse and the
singularity. An eclipse symbolizes the darkness that
precedes brilliance, while a singularity represents the point
where immense pressure transforms everything that
follows. Together, they embody the journey from
uncertainty to clarity, from potential to purpose.
At TELESTO 2026, every challenge is designed to mirror this
transformation. Participants are encouraged to embrace
complexity, make bold decisions under pressure, and
emerge stronger through every experience. The theme
celebrates resilience, conviction, and the pursuit of
excellence, reminding every participant that true
leadership is forged at the moments that define us.

Beyond every shadow lies the opportunity to shine.

OUR EVENTS
Telesto 2026 has one vision to challenge the brightest
young minds in business to thrive under pressure and
emerge stronger through uncertainty. Your background is
not a barrier. Your nationality is not a barrier. What matters
is your ability to navigate complexity, make decisive
choices, and lead when the stakes are highest.
This year's fest is built around The Noctis Singularity, a
theme inspired by the eclipse and the singularity, where
moments of darkness reveal brilliance and pressure
becomes the catalyst for transformation. It reflects the
journey of every leader who must cross their own horizon,
embracing uncertainty with conviction and turning
challenges into opportunities.
Every event, every round, is designed to simulate highstakes, real-world business challenges. No fillers. No
wasted potential. Only strategy, resilience, adaptability,
and leadership under pressure. At its core, Telesto is not
just about finding the right answers it is about rising
beyond uncertainty and discovering the leader you
become on the other side of it.
